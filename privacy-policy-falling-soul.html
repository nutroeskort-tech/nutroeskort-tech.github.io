Falling Soul

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Falling Soul, developed and published by Nutroeskort, handles user information.

Information We Collect

Falling Soul does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information. The game does not require users to create an account or register in order to play.

The game does not request or collect:

  • Full name or email address
  • Phone number or physical address
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, or personal media
  • Financial or payment information
  • Authentication credentials

Game Data and Progress

Game progress, settings, scores, and other gameplay-related information are stored locally on the user's device. Nutroeskort does not receive or maintain this information on its own servers.

Advertising, Analytics, and In-App Purchases

Falling Soul does not use advertising services or third-party analytics tools. The game does not contain in-app purchases.

Internet and Third-Party Services

The core game experience is designed to operate without requiring a user account or transmission of personal information to Nutroeskort. The app does not intentionally share personal information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Because Falling Soul does not knowingly collect personal information from users, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If the functionality of the game changes in a way that affects data handling, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Security

Nutroeskort minimizes privacy risks by not operating a system for collecting or storing users' personal information through the game.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's functionality or data practices change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Nutroeskort using the developer contact information provided on the relevant app store listing.
